Passport Photo Sheet

Turn any photo into a print-ready passport photo sheet — free, no account, no upload. Take a photo or upload one, and this auto-crops it to standard 2x2" head-size/eye-line proportions, can swap in a plain white background, and lays out a 4x6" sheet of 6 photos ready to print at home or at a photo kiosk (see the tip on the download step for a cheap print option).

🔒 Your photo never leaves your device — everything runs locally in your browser.
